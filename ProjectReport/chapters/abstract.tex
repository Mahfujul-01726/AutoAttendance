Image watermarking is a crucial technique for ensuring copyright protection and authentication in digital multimedia. This thesis proposes a hybrid approach to achieve secure and robust image watermarking through a combined framework utilizing Discrete Wavelet Transform (DWT), Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT), and Genetic Algorithm (GA). 

The objective is to enhance the imperceptibility of the embedded watermark while maintaining robustness against various attacks such as noise addition, compression, and geometric transformations. The proposed method leverages the multi-resolution capabilities of DWT for embedding the watermark in frequency sub-bands and employs DFT for frequency domain analysis to strengthen watermark imperceptibility. Genetic Algorithm is integrated to optimize the embedding and extraction process, thereby improving robustness against attacks.

Experimental evaluations demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed framework in achieving high fidelity and resilience to malicious attacks compared to existing watermarking techniques. This research contributes to the advancement of secure digital watermarking techniques by introducing a comprehensive framework that combines the strengths of DWT, DFT, and GA. The results achieved an average PSNR value of 41.67 dB which underscores its potential applicability in various domains requiring copyright protection and content authentication in digital multimedia.

\textbf{Keywords:} Image watermarking, Discrete Wavelet Transform, Discrete Fourier Transform, Genetic Algorithm, security, robustness.
