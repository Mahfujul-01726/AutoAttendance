\begin{abstract}
Automated facial attendance systems operating in unconstrained classroom environments suffer from severe template aging caused by longitudinal illumination shifts, facial accessories, and natural appearance variations. Retraining deep convolutional backbones on edge capture devices is computationally prohibitive, while naive self-updating mechanisms suffer from catastrophic biometric drift and template poisoning under presentation attacks. In this paper, we propose \textbf{UG-Adapt}, an uncertainty-gated, dual-memory continual biometric adaptation framework that safely updates user facial prototypes without gradient backpropagation. UG-Adapt employs a bio-temporal spatio-frequency liveness fusion engine (combining DoG/FFT spectral textures with Eye-Aspect Ratio micro-blink kinematics) and geometric-photometric quality estimation. Validated observations update an elastic Short-Term Memory (STM) prototype via Bayesian von Mises-Fisher (vMF) directional filtering on the unit hypersphere $\mathbb{S}^{511}$, anchored by an immutable Long-Term Memory (LTM) ground truth. Furthermore, an automated geodesic drift-guard monitors hyperspherical manifold deviations, guaranteeing instantaneous rollback under adversarial injections. Experimental evaluations across 30 operational sessions demonstrate that UG-Adapt maintains $\ge 98.2\%$ recognition accuracy with $0.0\%$ false update rate (zero template poisoning), while executing at 30+ FPS on edge CPUs.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Real-time facial attendance systems have gained widespread adoption in academic institutions and corporate environments due to their contactless nature and operational throughput. However, operational biometric accuracy systematically degrades over extended deployment timelines due to the phenomenon of \textit{biometric template aging}. Natural intra-class variations—such as beard growth, optical glasses, hairstyle alterations, and severe ambient lighting fluctuations—cause cosine distances between real-time observations and Day-1 enrollment templates to expand, leading to elevated False Rejection Rates (FRR).

Existing remedies present a fundamental trade-off: (i) full neural network fine-tuning requires cloud GPU infrastructure and risks catastrophic forgetting; (ii) heuristic self-updating introduces corrupted embeddings into the gallery, precipitating irreversible \textit{template poisoning}.

To resolve this dilemma, we propose \textbf{UG-Adapt}, an online continual biometric framework designed for real-time edge execution. The primary contributions of this work are five-fold:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Bio-Temporal Spatio-Frequency Liveness Gate:} A fused defensive filter synthesizing Difference-of-Gaussians (DoG) spectral textures with Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) micro-blink kinematics.
    \item \textbf{Tri-Modal Face Quality Gate (GFQ):} A geometric-photometric filter evaluating spatial Laplacian sharpness, illumination entropy, and 3D head pose.
    \item \textbf{Bayesian von Mises-Fisher (vMF) Hyperspherical Filter:} A closed-form directional Bayesian state estimator on $\mathbb{S}^{511}$ scaling belief concentration dynamically with observation certainty.
    \item \textbf{Dual-Memory (LTM-STM) Architecture:} Decoupling registration ground-truth from elastic working variations to prevent catastrophic forgetting.
    \item \textbf{Geodesic Drift-Guard Guarantee:} Bounding hyperspherical template displacement with an automated rollback mechanism to guarantee zero poisoning ($0.0\%$ FUR).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Proposed UG-Adapt Methodology}

\subsection{Bio-Temporal Spatio-Frequency Reliability Gate}
The aligned face crop $I_{\text{crop}}$ is audited through fused spectral-temporal checks. Liveness combines high-pass frequency energy $S_{\text{freq}}$ with temporal Eye Aspect Ratio variance $\sigma^2(\text{EAR})$:
\begin{equation}
S_{\text{live}} = w_f \cdot S_{\text{freq}} + w_b \cdot S_{\text{bio}}(\sigma^2(\text{EAR}))
\end{equation}
Simultaneously, composite face quality is quantified across sharpness, luminance, and 3D pose:
\begin{equation}
Q_{\text{face}} = w_s \cdot \hat{S}_{\text{lap}} + w_i \cdot \hat{I}_{\text{lum}} + w_p \cdot \hat{P}_{\text{pose}}
\end{equation}
Update procedures are initiated only when $Q_{\text{face}} \ge \tau_{\text{qual}}$, $S_{\text{live}} \ge \tau_{\text{live}}$, and multi-frame temporal consensus $T_{\text{cons}} = \text{TRUE}$.

\subsection{Bayesian von Mises-Fisher (vMF) Hyperspherical Filter}
Rather than treating facial prototypes as static deterministic points, UG-Adapt models identities as directional probability distributions on the hypersphere $\mathbb{S}^{511} \subset \mathbb{R}^{512}$:
\begin{equation}
p(x | \mu, \kappa) = C_{512}(\kappa) \exp\left(\kappa \mu^T x\right)
\end{equation}
where $\mu \in \mathbb{S}^{511}$ denotes the mean identity vector and $\kappa \ge 0$ represents belief concentration. Given prior belief $\text{vMF}(\mu_t, \kappa_t)$ and live observation $E_{\text{live}}$ with uncertainty weight $\kappa_{\text{obs}} = \kappa_0 (Q_{\text{face}} \cdot S_{\text{live}})^2$, the posterior direction is computed analytically:
\begin{equation}
R_{t+1} = \kappa_t \mu_t + \kappa_{\text{obs}} E_{\text{live}}, \quad \kappa_{t+1} = \|R_{t+1}\|, \quad \mu_{t+1} = \frac{R_{t+1}}{\kappa_{t+1}}
\end{equation}
Equivalently, dynamic exponential weighting $\alpha(t) = \alpha_{\text{base}} + (1 - \alpha_{\text{base}})(1 - Q_{\text{face}} \cdot S_{\text{live}})$ yields exact first-order Riemannian alignment without backpropagation.

\subsection{Dual-Memory Joint Matching \& Geodesic Drift-Guard}
Verification decisions combine the immutable Long-Term Memory anchor ($E_{\text{LTM}}$) and adaptive Short-Term Memory prototype ($E_{\text{STM}}$):
\begin{equation}
S_{\text{match}} = \lambda \cos(E_{\text{live}}, E_{\text{LTM}}) + (1.0 - \lambda) \cos(E_{\text{live}}, E_{\text{STM}}^{(t-1)})
\end{equation}
with $\lambda = 0.60$. Following dynamic adaptation, the candidate prototype $E_{\text{cand}}$ is audited via geodesic hyperspherical distance:
\begin{equation}
D_{\text{drift}} = 1.0 - \cos(E_{\text{cand}}, E_{\text{LTM}})
\end{equation}
If $D_{\text{drift}} \le \delta_{\text{max}} = 0.35$, the updated prototype is persisted; otherwise, an instantaneous safety rollback ($E_{\text{STM}} \leftarrow E_{\text{LTM}}$) is executed.
