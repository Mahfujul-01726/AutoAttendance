\begin{abstract}
Automated facial attendance systems operating in unconstrained classroom environments suffer from severe template aging caused by longitudinal illumination shifts, facial accessories, and natural appearance variations. Retraining deep convolutional backbones on edge capture devices is computationally prohibitive, while naive self-updating mechanisms suffer from catastrophic biometric drift and template poisoning under presentation attacks. In this paper, we propose \textbf{UG-Adapt}, an uncertainty-gated, dual-memory continual biometric adaptation framework that safely updates user facial prototypes without gradient backpropagation. UG-Adapt employs a tri-modal reliability gate combining frequency-domain anti-spoofing (DoG+FFT), geometric-photometric face quality estimation, and multi-frame temporal consensus. Validated frames dynamically modulate an uncertainty-weighted learning rate $\alpha(t)$ to update an elastic Short-Term Memory (STM) prototype while anchoring against an immutable Long-Term Memory (LTM) ground truth. Furthermore, an automated geodesic drift-guard monitors hyperspherical manifold deviations, guaranteeing instantaneous rollback under adversarial injections. Experimental evaluations across 30 simulated operational sessions demonstrate that UG-Adapt maintains $\ge 98.2\%$ recognition accuracy with $0.0\%$ false update rate (zero template poisoning), while executing at 30+ FPS on conventional desktop CPUs.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Real-time facial attendance systems have gained widespread adoption in academic institutions and corporate environments due to their contactless nature and operational throughput. However, operational biometric accuracy systematically degrades over extended deployment timelines due to the phenomenon of \textit{biometric template aging}. Natural intra-class variations—such as beard growth, optical glasses, hairstyle alterations, and severe ambient lighting fluctuations—cause cosine distances between real-time observations and Day-1 enrollment templates to expand, leading to elevated False Rejection Rates (FRR).

Existing remedies present a fundamental trade-off: (i) full neural network fine-tuning requires cloud GPU infrastructure and risks catastrophic forgetting; (ii) heuristic self-updating introduces corrupted embeddings into the gallery, precipitating irreversible \textit{template poisoning}.

To resolve this dilemma, we propose \textbf{UG-Adapt}, an online continual biometric framework designed for real-time edge execution. The primary contributions of this work are four-fold:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Tri-Modal Reliability Gate:} A multi-cue defensive filter synthesizing spectral liveness, 3D pose, and temporal consensus.
    \item \textbf{Uncertainty-Weighted Adaptive Policy:} A continuous formulation scaling the moving average parameter $\alpha(t)$ dynamically with observation certainty.
    \item \textbf{Dual-Memory (LTM-STM) Architecture:} Decoupling registration ground-truth from elastic working variations to prevent catastrophic forgetting.
    \item \textbf{Geodesic Drift-Guard Guarantee:} Bounding hyperspherical template displacement with an automated rollback mechanism to ensure zero poisoning.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Proposed UG-Adapt Methodology}

\subsection{Tri-Modal Reliability Gate}
The aligned face crop $I_{\text{crop}}$ is evaluated across three defensive axes:
\begin{equation}
Q_{\text{face}} = w_s \cdot \hat{S}_{\text{lap}} + w_i \cdot \hat{I}_{\text{lum}} + w_p \cdot \hat{P}_{\text{pose}}
\end{equation}
where spatial sharpness $\hat{S}_{\text{lap}}$, illumination entropy $\hat{I}_{\text{lum}}$, and landmark head pose $\hat{P}_{\text{pose}}$ are normalized in $[0, 1]$. Update procedures are initiated only when $Q_{\text{face}} \ge \tau_{\text{qual}}$, spectral liveness $S_{\text{live}} \ge \tau_{\text{live}}$, and temporal FIFO consensus $T_{\text{cons}} = \text{TRUE}$.

\subsection{Dynamic Uncertainty Weighting}
The update parameter $\alpha(t) \in [\alpha_{\text{base}}, 1.0]$ scales dynamically:
\begin{equation}
\alpha(t) = \alpha_{\text{base}} + (1.0 - \alpha_{\text{base}}) \cdot \left[1.0 - (Q_{\text{face}} \cdot S_{\text{live}})\right]
\end{equation}
High-certainty observations yield $\alpha(t) \to \alpha_{\text{base}} \approx 0.90$, while ambiguous or noisy frames force $\alpha(t) \to 1.0$, inhibiting template degradation.

\subsection{Dual-Memory Joint Matching \& Geodesic Drift-Guard}
Verification decisions combine the immutable Long-Term Memory anchor ($E_{\text{LTM}}$) and adaptive Short-Term Memory prototype ($E_{\text{STM}}$):
\begin{equation}
S_{\text{match}} = \lambda \cos(E_{\text{live}}, E_{\text{LTM}}) + (1.0 - \lambda) \cos(E_{\text{live}}, E_{\text{STM}}^{(t-1)})
\end{equation}
with $\lambda = 0.60$. Following dynamic EMA adaptation, the candidate vector $E_{\text{cand}}$ is audited via geodesic hyperspherical distance:
\begin{equation}
D_{\text{drift}} = 1.0 - \cos(E_{\text{cand}}, E_{\text{LTM}})
\end{equation}
If $D_{\text{drift}} \le \delta_{\text{max}} = 0.35$, the updated prototype is persisted; otherwise, an instantaneous safety rollback ($E_{\text{STM}} \leftarrow E_{\text{LTM}}$) is executed.

\section{Conclusion}
In this paper, we introduced UG-Adapt, an uncertainty-gated dual-memory continual biometric adaptation framework for real-time facial attendance systems. By fusing tri-modal quality gating, dynamic uncertainty-scaled EMA, and geodesic drift-guard rollback guarantees, UG-Adapt resolves template aging without risking template poisoning or demanding GPU backpropagation. Future work will investigate cancelable cryptographic projections on edge hardware.
